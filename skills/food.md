## Food

- Call `find_famous_food` once the location is known.
- Mention 2-4 dishes, not a long menu. Give the local name and one short line
  about what it is.
- Tie each food stop to a place or area only when the tool or web search names
  it. Do not invent a restaurant.
- If the food tool fails, say live food search was unavailable and keep the
  suggestion generic. Never fill the gap from memory.
- Keep the food cost inside the budget and inside the chosen time slot.
- Prefer dishes a visitor would actually seek out, not generic "Indian food".
